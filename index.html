PocketPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

PocketPlan keeps your money data on your phone. It has no account and no analytics, and your budgets and transactions are never sent anywhere. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses the internet.

What PocketPlan stores

Budgets, categories, transactions, wallets, bills, savings goals, receipt photos you attach and your settings. All of it is stored inside the app on your phone.

Ads

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. In the EU and UK, PocketPlan asks for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Ads never see your budgets or transactions. PocketPlan Pro has no ads.

Purchases

PocketPlan Pro (lifetime or yearly) and the earlier "Remove ads" are paid through Google Play. PocketPlan only learns whether your Google account owns them; your payment details stay with Google.

Backups

Android may include PocketPlan in your phone's own backup to your Google account (receipt photos are left out). If you turn on daily backup or export a backup, PocketPlan writes a file only to the place you choose, such as your phone or Google Drive. Those copies are handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

Notifications: for bill reminders, budget alerts and savings reminders. Camera and photos: only when you attach a receipt, using your phone's camera app and photo picker. App lock: fingerprint or screen lock is checked by Android; PocketPlan never sees it.

Sharing

PocketPlan shares nothing with anyone. A PDF report or backup leaves the app only when you share or save it yourself.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or uninstall PocketPlan to remove everything stored on the phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them.

Children

PocketPlan is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app with a new date.
